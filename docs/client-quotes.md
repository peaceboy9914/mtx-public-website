# Client quotes: approval kit

Each quote below is a **draft**. Send it to the client, get their reply in writing (WhatsApp or email is fine),
then update `src/content/clients.ts`: paste their final wording, fill in `name` and `role`, and set
`approved: true`. Nothing appears on the website until all three are done.

If the client prefers a line from their recommendation letter, use that instead: it is already their own words.

## Message to send

> Hello [name], thank you again for agreeing to a quote for our website. Here is a short draft based on the
> work we did together:
>
> "[quote]"
>
> Please feel free to change anything so it sounds like you, or reply "approved" if it's fine as it is.
> Could you also confirm the name and job title you'd like shown under it? Thank you!
